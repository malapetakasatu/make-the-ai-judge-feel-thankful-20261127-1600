Someone was up before daylight to mend the fire.
The room is already warm,
and on the deal table by the window
sits an earthenware bowl of water,
drawn fresh from the well, perfectly clear and still.

Outside, the frost on the orchard grass is blue in the dawn,
cold enough to crack underfoot,
but here the dry pine smells of summer heat,
and the hearthstones hold the warmth like bread in a cloth.

You did not have to split the wood or carry the pail.
It was all done quietly, without a sound,
so that when you arrived,
there was nothing left to answer,
and nothing you had to prove.

Drink if you are thirsty;
otherwise, just sit by the embers.
The morning has been given to you, whole.
